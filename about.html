01 / My story

From engineering foundations
to healthcare data.

A journey shaped by curiosity, a leap across continents, and a growing belief that reliable data can strengthen the systems people depend on.

My path into data was not a straight line. It began with engineering, expanded through graduate study, and found its purpose in healthcare and public health.

THE FOUNDATION

Building a Foundation in Engineering and Data

My journey into data began with a Bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering, where I developed a strong foundation in problem-solving, systems thinking, and technology.

During my undergraduate studies, I discovered my interest in data and analytics. That curiosity inspired me to pursue a Master’s degree in Data Science at Indiana University Bloomington.

THE LEAP

Taking a Leap of Faith

In 2023, I moved from India to the United States to pursue my graduate studies. It was one of the most challenging and rewarding decisions of my life.

Starting over in a new country pushed me beyond my comfort zone and strengthened my resilience. Being surrounded by new cultures, people, and ways of thinking has shaped how I see the world today.

THE PURPOSE

Finding Purpose in Healthcare Data

Today, I work in public health and healthcare analytics, helping organizations transform complex data into meaningful insights.

My work focuses on public-health surveillance, HL7 interoperability, ELR and eCR systems, patient identity resolution, and Snowflake-based data platforms.

What excites me most is the opportunity to build scalable data solutions that support better decisions, strengthen public-health systems, and ultimately create a positive impact on communities.
